SUMMER PROGRAM

Martial Arts Summer Camp

Our Martial Arts Summer Camp is the perfect way for kids to stay active, learn new skills, and make lasting friendships during the summer months. Each week-long session combines Taekwondo training with fitness activities, team-building exercises, and character development lessons.

It’s not just physical exercise — our summer camp includes exciting games, field trips, crafts, and dedicated study time to keep young minds sharp and engaged all summer long!

For more information about upcoming camp dates and registration, please contact us at (404) 477-5599 or moohansandysprings@gmail.com.

CORE CAMP HIGHLIGHTS

BENEFITS OF ACTION SUMMER CAMP IN SANDY SPRINGS

🎯

PHYSICAL FITNESS MASTERY

Participants in our Moohan Summer Camp will experience a significant boost in physical fitness. Through engaging martial arts training, children will enhance their strength, flexibility, coordination, and cardiovascular health. The camp provides a fun and dynamic environment where kids can develop lifelong habits of staying active and healthy.

🤝

DISCIPLINE AND FOCUS

Martial arts instill important values like discipline, focus, and respect. Our summer camp integrates these principles into daily activities, helping children improve their concentration, self-control, and goal-setting abilities. Through the structured and positive learning environment of martial arts, participants develop essential life skills that extend beyond the camp experience.

📊

CONFIDENCE BUILDING & CHARACTER DEVELOPMENT

Martial arts empower kids by building their self-confidence and self-esteem. Moohan summer camp emphasizes character development through activities that encourage teamwork, leadership, and positive social interactions. As children progress in their martial arts skills, they gain a sense of accomplishment, resilience, and a belief in their own capabilities.

MORE THAN JUST PHYSICAL EXERCISE

MOOHAN SUMMER CAMP program is simply the best in town!

Moohan Martial Arts Summer Camp offers a safe, exciting, and educational experience your children will never forget. Our camp includes physical fitness, team sports, games, outdoor field trips, creative arts & crafts, and dedicated study time!

FUN, SAFE, & EDUCATIONAL

Whether you are signing up for one week or the whole summer, Action Summer Camps offer a safe, exciting, and educational environment for children. Our camp offers a unique experience that makes sure your child is having fun, continuously learning, and building positive character traits.

TEAM SPORTS, GAMES, & CRAFTS

Games at our camp are endless! There are so many exciting games and activities that it is impossible to list them all. We engage in lots of games (such as archery, laser tag, obstacle course, dance, soccer, etc.) that are designed to enhance cooperation, teamwork, and leadership skills. We also have arts & crafts that help develop fine motor skills and creativity as well as the joy of accomplishment. Everyone is sure to have a GREAT time.

FIELD TRIPS & OUTDOOR ADVENTURES

Campers look forward to exciting field trips and outdoor adventures! We take fun trips to local parks, recreation spots, and educational venues, giving kids a chance to explore, socialize, and build unforgettable summer memories together in a supervised, safe environment.

STUDY TIME & MENTAL DISCIPLINE

We balance high-energy physical fun with structured quiet study time! Campers have dedicated reading and study sessions every day to keep their academic skills sharp over the break, fostering good habits, focus, and a healthy balance between body and mind.

MARTIAL ARTS TRAINING

Students will enjoy learning a variety of punching and kicking techniques from different martial arts forms along with a chance to try them out on various pieces of safe training equipment. They will learn self-defense skills and participate in martial arts games. Whether from our reflex-sharpening drills or our obstacle course, students receive plenty of exercise and will be ready at the end of the day to spend quality time with family. Training in our combination martial arts provides a constructive outlet for the extra energy children normally have while promoting health, leadership, and physical fitness.
