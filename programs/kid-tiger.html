PROGRAM OVERVIEW

Kid Tiger Program (Ages 4-5)

The Kids Tiger Program, designed for children aged 4 to 5, focuses on developing confidence, focus, and physical coordination through tailored Taekwondo classes. In a fun and safe environment, children learn basic Taekwondo techniques while also building essential life skills like discipline and respect. This program encourages active learning and helps young kids grow both mentally and physically in a structured, positive setting.

KEY BENEFITS

Why Choose Taekwondo for Your Child?

🎯

Improves Focus and Concentration

Taekwondo requires children to pay attention to instructions and master specific techniques, which helps improve their focus and concentration both in class and in everyday activities.

⭐

Builds Confidence

As children progress through the ranks and achieve new belts, they experience a sense of accomplishment that boosts their self-esteem and confidence in their abilities.

🤝

Teaches Discipline and Respect

Taekwondo instills core values like discipline, respect for others, and self-control, helping children learn how to follow directions and treat peers and teachers with respect.

Taekwondo for Kids 4-5

Taekwondo offers significant benefits for children aged 4-5, focusing on their physical, mental, and emotional development. At this age, kids are rapidly developing motor skills, and Taekwondo's structured movements help improve coordination and balance. The practice also enhances focus and listening skills, teaching young children to follow directions and concentrate on tasks. Additionally, Taekwondo builds self-confidence as children achieve new milestones, encouraging a positive sense of accomplishment and personal growth.

✓
Enhances Motor Skills: Improves coordination, balance, and fine motor skills.
✓
Encourages Focus and Listening: Teaches children to follow directions and concentrate.
✓
Builds Self-Confidence: Fosters a sense of achievement as kids master new skills.

Moohan is Korean for Infinity or Unlimited. This represents our belief that the study of Martial Arts is a never ending process of growth and improvement for both children and adults. It is our mission to bring an understanding of the ways of traditional martial arts to all people. Through our programs of excellence, we will teach skills and values that can be used in all aspects of life.
