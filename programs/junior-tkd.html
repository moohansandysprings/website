PROGRAM OVERVIEW

Junior Taekwondo Program (Ages 6-12)

Empower your child ages 6–12 with our Junior Taekwondo Program in Sandy Springs, Georgia. Through structured martial arts training, students build confidence, discipline, and physical fitness in a fun and supportive environment.

Our experienced instructors help children develop focus, respect, and leadership while progressing at their own pace through goal-setting and belt advancement.

New and experienced students are welcome. Enroll today for a FREE trial class and watch your child grow.

KEY BENEFITS

Why Taekwondo for Kids Ages 6-12?

⭐

Build Confidence

As children progress through belt ranks and set personal goals, they gain a sense of achievement that boosts their self-esteem. Success in Taekwondo encourages them to believe in their abilities, both in martial arts and other areas of life.

💪

Improve Physical Fitness

Taekwondo provides a full-body workout that enhances strength, flexibility, coordination, and endurance. Regular practice helps kids stay active and develop lifelong healthy habits.

🧠

Develop Focus and Discipline

Learning Taekwondo requires concentration, discipline, and perseverance. These skills not only improve performance in martial arts but also positively impact academic success and personal growth.

Taekwondo for Kids 6-12 years old

Taekwondo offers significant benefits for children aged 6-12, focusing on their physical, mental, and emotional development. At this age, kids are building strength and refining motor skills, and Taekwondo’s dynamic techniques help improve coordination, flexibility, and endurance. The practice also enhances focus and discipline, teaching children to set goals, follow instructions, and concentrate on tasks. Additionally, Taekwondo builds self-confidence as children progress through belt levels, encouraging a positive sense of achievement and personal growth in a supportive environment.

✓
Increased Self-Discipline: Manages impulses and builds structured daily routines.
✓
Respect for Peers and Teachers: Fosters positive behavior and social respect.
✓
Better Focus and Goal Setting: Translates to improved schoolwork concentration.

Moohan is Korean for Infinity or Unlimited. This represents our belief that the study of Martial Arts is a never ending process of growth and improvement for both children and adults. It is our mission to bring an understanding of the ways of traditional martial arts to all people. Through our programs of excellence, we will teach skills and values that can be used in all aspects of life.
